## 🔍 Overview
The computational protocol simulates proteins positioned near lipid surfaces modeled at low temperatures. This approach mimics membrane-like environments under cryogenic conditions, replicating the temperature-controlled settings of laboratory experiments. By running simulations under NPT ensemble conditions with Langevin dynamics, the protocol accurately reproduces the thermal and pressure fluctuations experienced in experimental cryogenic studies. This setup enables detailed investigation of protein–lipid interactions at low temperatures, providing atomic-level view of intermolecular interactions.
